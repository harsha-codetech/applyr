applyr is installed

Fill job applications from one profile. Nothing leaves this browser — no account, no server, no analytics.

Three things to know

  1. You always submit. applyr fills the form and rings every field it touched. Reviewing and clicking Submit stays with you — that is deliberate, not a missing feature.
  2. It gets smarter as you use it. Anything it cannot place, you can teach once. The next employer who asks the same question in different words gets the same answer automatically.
  3. Your data is only here. Profile, résumés and saved answers live in this browser profile. Export a backup from Settings — there is no cloud copy to fall back on.

Get started

  1. Open the side panel from the toolbar icon, or with the button below.
  2. Fill in your profile — name, contact, links, work history.
  3. Upload a résumé under Documents. PDF parses best.
  4. Open any job application on Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby and the other supported boards, then press Alt+Shift+F or use the panel button.

On a site applyr does not ship a pack for, it falls back to generic matching and usually still fills most of the form. Grant access per-site from the panel when you want that.
